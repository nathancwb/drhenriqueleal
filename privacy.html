2. Principles and Professional Confidentiality

The processing of health data and personal information is governed by the ethical standards of the professional councils (CRO-PR and CRBM-PR) and by Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Lei nº 13.709/2018). All information shared by patients for clinical assessment, scheduling, or treatment is handled under strict professional secrecy and medical/dental confidentiality.

3. Data Collected and Purposes

We collect only the data strictly necessary to provide safe, quality care to patients:

  • Identification and Contact Data: Full name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and email, collected when a user reaches out to schedule an appointment or ask a question.
  • Health and Clinical Record Data: Health history, allergies, previous procedures, and clinical photos, collected only in person during a clinical consultation, for planning and safely performing procedures.
  • Browsing and Cookie Data: Approximate IP address and anonymous analytics on browsing and site performance, gathered to improve the usability of the website.

4. Sharing and Secure Storage

The practice does not sell, rent, or share patients' personal data with third parties for advertising purposes. Data is shared only with providers of essential, secure technology infrastructure (for example, servers with SSL/TLS encryption, certified cloud scheduling systems, and diagnostic support labs, when expressly authorized by the patient).

5. Data Subject Rights (LGPD)

Under Article 18 of Law No. 13.709/2018 (LGPD), you may request at any time:

  • Confirmation that your data is being processed;
  • Transparent access to your registered data;
  • Correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
  • Anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data;
  • Withdrawal of consent for promotional or informational communications.

6. Cookies and Measurement Technologies

We use essential technical cookies for the site to function, and anonymous analytics cookies to understand visitor flow and improve our educational content on facial harmonization and aesthetics. You can disable or manage cookies directly in your web browser's settings.
